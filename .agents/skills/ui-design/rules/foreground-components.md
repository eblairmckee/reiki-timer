# Recessed wells and frosted touch surfaces

The [composed reference](../../../../docs/concepts/mobile-timer-concepts/timer-config--amber-relief--rest--v01.png), [dial](../../../../docs/concepts/mobile-timer-concepts/dial--recessed-frost--rest-hover-press--v03.png), [switch](../../../../docs/concepts/mobile-timer-concepts/switch--recessed-frost--off-rest-hover-press--v01.png) and [HUD](../../../../docs/concepts/mobile-timer-concepts/hud-start--recessed-frost--rest-hover-press--v01.png) establish material and silhouette. Current [type](typography.md), [layout](layout-and-content.md) and [palette](atmosphere.md) rules supersede those images' lettering, large readout and pastel colors.

Use neutral translucent pigments: dark inset shading for recessed wells, light fill and bevel for frosted touch surfaces, and restrained contact shadows. Static backdrop blur carries the actual local chakra color through the material, including cool and mixed backgrounds. Avoid warm-tinted frost, opaque cream fills, extra outline strokes and floating card containers. The switch track/thumb define the edge relationship.

- **Icons.** Use Hugeicons through `@hugeicons/react` and `@hugeicons/core-free-icons` for interface glyphs. Reuse the app’s semantic icon atoms where available. Give each action either a visible text label or a single icon. When an icon action needs explanation, place it in nearby descriptive copy. Keep decorative glyphs hidden from assistive technology and give their controls accessible action names. Use a consistent 1.5px stroke at the base 24px size. Playback glyphs can use a filled face for clarity. Draw continuous scales and tick marks as control geometry.
- **Dial.** Use a frosted disk with a short etched index and fixed marks appropriate to the range. The session dial marks five-minute intervals and emphasizes quarter hours. The ten-minute warm-up and cooldown dials mark each minute. Preserve one-minute pointer and keyboard selection, the pressure halo and the release wave. Follow [layout](layout-and-content.md) for the outside value and field sizing.
- **Switch:** fixed recessed track and moving frosted thumb; position communicates state. Pressure outlines the capsule track; the release splash is circular and follows the thumb as it settles.
- **HUD actions.** Anchor the primary control at the right edge. A lone action is a fully round frosted button. Center its shorter recessed summary vertically and overlap just its trailing edge, with enough padding to keep every item readable. The summary stays informational and can unfold its timeline on hover or keyboard focus. For multiple actions, compose the [Olyx ButtonGroup and ButtonGroupSeparator](../../../../src/components/ui/button-group/index.tsx) through the [HUD molecule](../../../../src/components/molecules/button-group/index.tsx). Give every segment a frosted face, a denser fill on the primary, square touching edges and full rounding at the two outer ends. Mark each join with a narrow shadow and a lit edge. Keep the primary hit target anchored as the group grows or contracts. Preserve individual accessible names, keyboard focus, pressure feedback, and mixed-case labels without underlines.
- **Action hierarchy.** Give one primary action the brightest frosted face and a lit upper edge. Use the word-button fill and dark ink for its copy. Give the secondary action a shallow rectangular well with a lit lower rim. Follow [typography](typography.md) for the pressed label. On press its inset edge deepens. Use the ghost button for a quiet secondary action over the atmosphere. Keep its label legible on a transparent surface with no underline at rest, on hover, on press or on focus. Use a subtle surface tint for pointer feedback and an outline for keyboard focus. Give every action a comfortable hit area. Word buttons use rectangular faces with rounded corners. Reserve circles for standalone icons. Button owns these variants. Inputs have their own state and material contracts.
- **Finish dialog.** Finish and natural expiry open the same “Reiki off” dialog over playback. Use a clear blurred backdrop and a translucent glass face with a single fine highlight tapering around the upper corner. Keep the title and description close together. Set Save and Edit as distinct, semibold inline actions in the description with visible keyboard focus. Leave without saving is the only filled word button, separated by a larger gap. Focus the heading on open. Dismissing returns focus to Finish.
- **Chime choices.** Group the six bell buttons under one visible sound label. Match the diameter of each visible tone face to the switch thumb at the same viewport size. Share its control sizing, frost and interactive lighting. Give the selected tone a denser fill and a persistent pressure ring with clearance between sibling rings. Keep accessible sound names and keyboard previews. A new timer uses the second tone. Show each phase-end cue only while its Warm up or Cool down phase is enabled. Preserve the tone and cue choices across toggles. Enabling Chimes with no available cue selected selects chakra changes alone. Turning off the last available selected cue collapses Chimes, switches it off and returns focus to its switch. Removing the phase with the last available selected cue also switches off Chimes, with focus staying on the phase switch.
- **Input/select:** style placeholder, value, icon, menu, options and selection as one material system, with a readable backing for the portal.

Keep Olyx/Base UI semantics, cancellation, focus, dismissal and collision handling. Cover rest, hover, press, focus, selected/open, disabled/read-only and invalid states where applicable. Disabled controls stay still. Compare edges, clearances, etching and typography over warm and cool palettes; automated behavior checks cannot prove visual parity.
