# Layout and spacing

## Space sets the pace

Use the system's spacing rhythm to distinguish groups and give priority content room. Keep comparison areas compact enough to scan. Let content determine breakpoints and dimensions; do not impose one density on every surface.

Review narrow and wide views, long labels, enlarged text, wrapping and meaningful content order. Check clipped actions, accidental page overflow and essential content hidden to fit. Use real content and native scroll behavior; a fitting screenshot does not establish all text sizes.

Keep reusable geometry with its component and product arrangement with the feature. Public styling extensions must be intentional contracts, not reliance on private selectors.
