# Hierarchy and meaning

Use for content priority, disclosure, grouping or orientation. These adapt the owner's principles to the current product; do not import investor-specific copy or requirements into unrelated UI.

## The deal, then reveal

Show essentials first with a clear path to details. Keep information needed for the current decision visible. Review whether disclosure reduces clutter without hiding required context or actions; essential meaning must not require hover.

## Close means connected

Keep a value, its explanation and related actions together. Separate unrelated groups through spacing. Review whether proximity and reading order reflect the task rather than incidental component boundaries.

## Name the frame. Keep the lens visible

Make the active scope, mode and relevant filters obvious. Review whether users can tell what they are viewing or changing after navigation and state transitions. Add dates/ranges only when they affect interpretation.

## Consistent cues, familiar views

Keep labels, formats and interaction patterns consistent when their meaning is the same. Preserve intentional differences. Product copy/localization stays with the feature; shared UI accepts the caller's meaning.
