# Instruction content

A reusable instruction needs a clear task, an established requirement or demonstrated failure mechanism, and an observable outcome. Keep one-off task details beside that task. Distinguish requirements from preferences and examples.

State the intended action directly, using concrete terms and the present tense. Write short, specific descriptions that select the skill for its actual work. Put procedure and conditions in the body or the relevant topic file. Remove repeated advice, slogans, praise, filler, and defensive wording; every instruction should change a decision or prevent a concrete error.

When direction changes, rewrite or remove the affected guidance and update its callers. Resolve contradictions at their owner. Active instructions contain the current behavior and any operational reason needed to apply it. Keep explanations of reversals, former approaches, conversations, and authorship in Git history or requested audit documentation.

Retain restrictions that protect a real contract, user choice, or fragile operation. Give ordinary implementation decisions room for judgment, and keep the workflow within the user's requested scope.
